## 1. Overview & Transparency Commitment

PRAGATI AI is an industrial energy intelligence and decision-support platform designed to reduce utility expenditure, peak demand surcharges, and Scope 2 carbon emissions in heavy industrial facilities.

To ensure **absolute scientific integrity and truth-in-advertising**:
- All calculations, mathematical algorithms, optimization formulations, and statutory tax treatments documented here reflect the **actual codebase** running in this repository.
- We strictly distinguish between **automated-test-verified code**, **independent mathematical benchmarks**, **externally verified facts/standards**, and **untested real-world pilot assumptions**.
- Data generated from the synthetic Faridabad steel plant benchmark fixture (`STEEL_BENCHMARK_FIXTURE_V1`) is explicitly labeled as **synthetic benchmark data**, not live hardware telemetry from an unverified physical factory.
- Financial metrics (NPV, IRR, payback) are **modeled engineering projections** under user-configurable parameters, not guaranteed commercial yields or formal tax opinions.

## 3. Provenance Hierarchy & Definitions

To prevent ambiguity, PRAGATI AI uses a 5-tier evidence hierarchy:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Level 5: Real-World Pilot Evidence (Awaiting Live Hardware) │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Level 4: External-Source Verification (CEA v19, Sec 32, BEE)│
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Level 3: Independent Mathematical Validation (Hand/Script)   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Level 2: Automated Unit & Integration Tests (148 Passing)   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Level 1: Implemented Software Capability                    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Level 1 — Implemented Software Capability**: The algorithm or feature exists in the Python codebase and can be executed via API or CLI.
2. **Level 2 — Automated Test Evidence**: The capability is covered by regression tests in `backend/tests/` that run in CI/CD and pass without errors.
3. **Level 3 — Independent Mathematical Validation**: The calculations have been verified against separate, hand-calculated first-principles scripts or external solvers (e.g. `test_independent_financial_benchmarks.py`).
4. **Level 4 — External-Source Verification**: Grounded in recognized statutory standards, regulatory publications, or peer-reviewed scientific literature (e.g., CEA CO2 Database v19, Indian Income Tax Act 1961 Section 32(1)).
5. **Level 5 — Real-World Pilot Evidence**: Validated against physical factory hardware sensors and commercial utility billing statements. *(Currently marked as "Awaiting Real-World Validation" for PRAGATI AI).*

## 4. Disclaimers and Non-Claims

1. **No Live Hardware Telemetry Claimed**: Telemetry data shown in the demonstration comes from the historical UCI Steel Industry Dataset and synthetic benchmark fixture `STEEL_BENCHMARK_FIXTURE_V1`. It is not claimed to be a live physical hardware feed from an active manufacturing facility.
2. **No Guaranteed Commercial Returns**: Financial payback periods, NPV figures, and operational cost savings are forward-looking engineering estimates computed under user-supplied assumptions. Actual savings will vary based on weather, unpredicted outages, tariff revisions, and equipment performance.
3. **No Tax Advice**: Discussions of Indian Income Tax Act Section 32 40% WDV depreciation are mathematical representations of statutory provisions and do not constitute formal tax or legal advice. Commercial deployment requires assessment by qualified tax professionals.
